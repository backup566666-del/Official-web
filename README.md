# Free City Story Hub

Create a premium, cinematic public homepage for FREE CITY ORGANIZATION, an open-world RPG roleplay gaming community.



VISUAL DESIGN:



Create a realistic, dark modern city at night as the main background. Include tall illuminated skyscrapers, palm trees, realistic city streets, dramatic streetlights, reflections on the road and subtle neon purple, pink and electric-blue lighting.



The scene must look like a professionally rendered open-world video game environment, with realistic 3D graphics and cinematic lighting. Do not make it look like an AI cartoon, anime, ordinary stock photograph or generic futuristic illustration.



HERO COMPOSITION:



- Place one realistic male and one realistic female 3D roleplay character together on one side.

- Their appearance should resemble detailed characters from a high-quality open-world game such as GTA Online, without copying specific characters.

- On the opposite side, feature a beautiful yellow Lamborghini-style luxury supercar.

- Blend the characters, car and dark city background into one cohesive cinematic scene.



HOMEPAGE CONTENT:



Main heading: FREE CITY ORGANIZATION



Tagline: YOUR CITY. YOUR STORY.



Supporting text: Enter the world of Free City Roleplay. Build your character, discover the city, meet people and create your own story.



Buttons:



- JOIN OUR DISCORD

- EXPLORE FREE CITY



Navigation:

Home, About, Community, Server Rules, Events, Login.



DESIGN DETAILS:



- Use a premium dark interface with restrained neon purple and electric-blue accents.

- Make the city background fixed and consistent while scrolling.

- Add subtle cinematic lighting and smooth animations.

- Use elegant typography and clean spacing.

- Make the website fully responsive, especially on Android phones.

- Do not create a logo yet. Do not display a random F icon.

- Do not invent statistics, events, membership prices or Discord information.



IMPORTANT:

Build the actual website homepage, not a presentation or a collection of static mockup images. Create functional navigation and buttons, using placeholders for links that have not yet been supplied.



Focus first on making the homepage visually impressive and realistic.
